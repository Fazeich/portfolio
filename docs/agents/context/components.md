# Component Context

## Page (`src/pages`)

- **PortfolioPage** (`src/pages/Portfolio`): Route `/` — a bright "diorama" top-down town (react-three-fiber). A switchable voxel character (mage or car) moves freely in a room with multiple altar signs; approaching an altar highlights it (pulsing accent ring + light), shows a tooltip, pressing E triggers a transition (the camera slowly dollies to the altar name while the screen fades to white), then navigates to the altar's target route.
  - `lib/TownScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — sky/fog atmosphere, hemisphere + warm sun + cool rim lights, drei `SoftShadows`, ground, fence, hills, stones, trees, gravel, lanterns, the 3×3 crate stack, all `ALTARS`, the active character, follow camera. Renders `ALTARS.map` into `AltarSign` with the `active` flag from `state.hoveredAltarId`, and renders `CharacterModel` (mage) or `CarModel` (car) depending on the `character` prop.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: `AltarDef` interface + `ALTARS` data array (id, position, billboard text, tooltip label/keyHint, altar AABB half-sizes, target route, `accent` color — snake `#22c55e`, letters `#f59e0b`). Visual/atmosphere tokens (sky/fog colors & range, sun position, lantern positions/pole size), `PLAYER_MARGIN` (0.7 — how close a character gets to the boundary fence), and camera tuning (base FOV, near-altar FOV kick, blend speeds, interaction zoom values).
  - `lib/Ground.tsx`: Near-infinite floor — a single large plane (2000×2000) with a procedural noise texture (canvas-generated, `RepeatWrapping`) and a plastic-like `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (roughness + clearcoat).
  - `lib/Walls.tsx`: Boundary **fence** around the walkable room (60×40) — wooden posts every ~4.4 units plus two horizontal rails on each edge (`#a9835c`), making the arena edge visible. Player movement is clamped right up to the fence via `PLAYER_MARGIN` (0.7), so there is no invisible wall.
  - `lib/Hills.tsx`: A ring of ~26 low-poly icosahedron hills (radius ~118–162, `#b9b2a5`) beyond the room — silhouettes that dissolve into the fog horizon.
  - `lib/Trees.tsx`: 10 low-poly trees (cylindrical trunk + icosahedron crown, `#5d7a52`) in a band just outside the walls, seeded layout.
  - `lib/Lanterns.tsx`: 4 corner lanterns inside the room (thin pole + emissive warm globe `#ffcf8a` + warm `pointLight`) — Bloom accents that also light the room.
  - `lib/Stones.tsx`: Decorative rocks scattered in a band just beyond the walls (seeded pseudo-random layout, 48 low-poly icosahedrons, `#8a857e` stone material, cast/receive shadow).
  - `lib/Gravel.tsx`: Gravel inside the room — a single `InstancedMesh` pool (900 dodecahedron pebbles, `#a89f92`-family shades) with per-instance transform/color, seeded layout, clear radius around each altar.
  - `lib/Crates.tsx`: 9 wooden crates in front of the spawn (near the center), **initially stacked** 2×2 on the ground + 2×2 on top + 1 crown. Hand-rolled 2.5D physics in a ref: gravity + support detection keep the stack up; on character contact a block pops away (horizontal impulse scaled by the character's measured speed + upward pop, slight random scatter + spin); toppled blocks fall, land on the ground or other crates (vertical overlap resolution), bounce off the boundary fence, and settle back into piles. Only ground-level crates collide with the character (`CHARACTER_REACH`). Character collision radius is passed in (car vs mage).
  - `lib/AltarSign.tsx`: Data-driven interaction target — a three-tier stone altar (3 stacked boxes, `#6b655c`) topped with an extruded 3D billboard text (`altar.text`). Text is drei `Text3D` (bundled `helvetiker_bold.typeface.json` font in `src/lib/assets/fonts/`, peach `#FED6BC`, no emissive), wrapped in `Billboard` + `Center`. When `active`: a pulsing accent-colored ground ring + accent point light (via `useFrame`).
  - `lib/CharacterModel.tsx`: Procedural **voxel scholar-mage** (character `mage`) built entirely from boxes/spheres in code, uniformly scaled `CHARACTER_SCALE` = 1.4. Silhouette: bell-shaped indigo robe (3 stacked skirt boxes + gold belt), dark torso, hooded head with glowing amber eyes, swinging sleeves with skin hands, and a small book held in the left hand. Fully hand-rolled animation in `useFrame`: body bob, skirt sway, forward torso lean, counter-swaying head, arm/forearm swing, and alternating foot steps (each limb is a pivot `group` rotating around its joint axis); gentle idle sway when standing. Movement clamps to the room and resolves obstacle collisions via the shared `physics.ts`; interactions run through the shared `interaction.ts` helpers.
  - `lib/CarModel.tsx`: Procedural **voxel car** (character `car`) — elongated red box body, hood, dark cabin with windows, glowing headlights, and 4 spinning wheels (front pair steer visually). Arcade-realistic physics in a ref: throttle/brake/reverse, rolling resistance, speed-scaled steering (no turning at standstill, reverse steering flips, A turns left), banking roll and acceleration pitch on the body, wheel spin from distance traveled, and speed damping on wall/obstacle hits. Shared room clamp + obstacle collision + interaction helpers.
  - `lib/CameraRig.tsx`: Smooth top-down follow camera (exponential lerp on position and look target). Near an altar: raises the camera and kicks FOV 45→52. While interacting: **slowly dollies toward the altar's billboard name** (much slower blend, `INTERACTION_CAMERA_SPEED` 0.3) and narrows the FOV slightly for a cinematic feel. Writes `state.hoveredAltarId` and fills the tooltip (label, key hint, target route) from the nearest altar within `INTERACTION_RADIUS`.
  - `lib/state.ts`: Mutable game state ref (player position/facing/walkPhase/targetWalkPhase, interaction timer, `interactionTarget`, `hoveredAltarId`, tooltip) + `CharacterId` type (`"mage" | "car"`). Initial `facing` is `Math.PI` so the character starts looking **away** from the follow camera.
  - `lib/controls.ts`: Keyboard input (WASD/arrows for movement/steering, E for interaction).
  - `ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`: DOM overlay above the hovered altar — ref-driven via rAF (no React re-renders).
  - `ui/HubHud.tsx`: DOM overlay — "PORTFOLIO" title + "3D mini-games" subtitle (top-left) and a WASD / E / 1 controls hint (bottom-center: "движение", "вход в проект", "переключение персонажа"). `pointer-events: none`.
  - `ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`: Canvas (fog/sky handled in the scene) + `EffectComposer` (soft `Bloom` + `Vignette`) + HubHud + tooltip + fade overlay + navigation callback. Holds the `character` state (**default `car`**); the **Digit1** key toggles it between mage and car. The fade overlay **slowly brightens to white** during activation (starts at ~25% of the animation, fully white by ~95%) then the route navigates.
- **LettersPage** (`src/pages/Letters`): Route `/letters` — Letter Rain. A full-screen 3D canvas; typing any printable character **anywhere on the page** spawns a single 3D letter above the top boundary. It falls and collides with the side/bottom bounds and with other letters (top stays open). The field is one row deep (`AREA_HALF_D` = 1.5 × letter depth) — letters are locked at z = 0 and pile into a single row. Letters never rotate around Y — they only tilt, and the tilt is **free and dynamic**: letters keep their landed angle, wobble on impact (no restore to a fixed flat pose). Each letter is at least 120×120 px on screen (recomputed from viewport/camera on resize). Cap: `MAX_LETTERS` (9) — the oldest letter is evicted when full.
  - `ui/LettersPage.tsx`: Full-screen layout. Mutable world in a ref; subscribes `letterTyped`/`clearLetters` Effector events and bumps a React counter so the letter list re-renders only on spawn/evict. A single window `keydown` handler (no input field): printable chars → `letterTyped`, Escape → back to the town. A fixed "← Назад" pill button (top-left) navigates back to `/`.
  - `ui/LettersScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — background color, ambient/directional/point lights, floor plane, translucent bounds walls (front/back at ± letter depth), `Letters`, the physics game loop, the camera rig, and Bloom. Camera fits the play box to the viewport each frame and writes `pxPerWorld`/`letterScale` into the world.
  - `ui/Letters.tsx`: One `<mesh>` per letter (drei-style `TextGeometry` from the shared font cache), memoized and keyed by id — old letters are never re-mounted/re-created when new ones spawn, only their transforms are synced from the world each frame. Uses a shared `MeshStandardMaterial` with a soft emissive for Bloom.
- **SnakePage** (`src/pages/Snake`): Route `/snake` — the 3D Snake game. Composes the 3D `<Canvas>` (Arena, Snake, Food, Shards, Effects, CameraRig, lights, Bloom) with DOM overlays (Hud, Screens).
  - `ui/GameLoop.tsx`: Orchestrates the game loop inside Canvas — resets the world when the store's `gameId` changes (new game only, not on resume), steps physics, dispatches score/HP/boost events.
  - `ui/PointerTracker.tsx`: Raycasts the mouse pointer onto the arena floor plane and writes the target point into `inputRef` for steering. Mouse steering is active **only while the left mouse button is held** and is **inverted** (the snake steers away from the cursor); releasing LMB stops steering (WASD keeps working).

## Feature Components (`src/components`)

- **Arena** (`src/components/Arena`): Renders the 3D arena — neon-grid floor (drei `Grid`) and 4 semi-transparent walls with glowing edges.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Wall thickness.
- **Snake** (`src/components/Snake`): Renders the snake as an `InstancedMesh` pool (head + chain), synced from world state each frame. Per-segment colors (head→tail gradient) and orientation.
  - `lib/render.ts`: Per-frame matrix/color sync for the instanced chain. Unused instances (beyond the current segment count) are scaled to 0 every frame to keep them invisible.
- **Food** (`src/components/Food`): Renders "protected" food — translucent shell sphere with a glowing core. Pool of `MAX_FOODS` slots synced from world state.
- **Shards** (`src/components/Shards`): Renders shard pickups as a single `InstancedMesh` octahedron pool (1 draw call). Per-instance colors distinguish edible (amber, from theme) from inert just-broken (gray).
- **CameraRig** (`src/components/CameraRig`): Follow camera behind the head with smoothing, FOV kick on boost, orbit mode on the menu screen.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Camera offsets, FOV values, orbit params.
- **Effects** (`src/components/Effects`): Ambient dust points + a particle burst pool triggered on shell breaks.
  - `lib/particles.ts`: CPU particle simulation (positions/colors/lifetimes, circle sprite texture).
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Particle counts, speed, lifetime.
- **Hud** (`src/components/Hud`): DOM overlay — score, best score, HP hearts, boost bar. The boost bar is ref-driven (updated in a `requestAnimationFrame` loop straight from the mutable world, no React re-renders) and exposed as `role="progressbar"`; hearts carry an `aria-label`. The boost bar dims and shows a `КД X.Xс` countdown while the boost cooldown is active.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for the HUD.
- **Screens** (`src/components/Screens`): DOM overlays for `menu` / `paused` / `gameover` phases — title, rules, controls, play buttons, social links (react-social-icons). Handles Esc-pause and Space/Enter-start hotkeys. Dialogs use `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal` and a Tab focus trap (`useFocusTrap`). Each panel has a "Назад" button that resets to the menu (`toMenu`) and navigates back to `/`.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for screens/panels/buttons.
