# Component Context

## Page (`src/pages`)

- **PortfolioPage** (`src/pages/Portfolio`): Route `/` — a bright "diorama" top-down town (react-three-fiber). A switchable voxel character (mage or car) moves freely in a room with multiple altar signs; approaching an altar highlights it (pulsing accent ring + light), shows a tooltip, pressing E triggers a transition (the camera slowly dollies to the altar name while the screen fades to white), then navigates to the altar's target route.
  - `lib/TownScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — sky/fog atmosphere, hemisphere + warm sun + cool rim lights, drei `SoftShadows`, `Terrain`, `Props`, `Pedestals`, the 3×3 crate stack, the active character, follow camera. Renders `Pedestals` with the mutable town state, and `CharacterModel` (mage) or `CarModel` (car) depending on the `character` prop. There is **no fence** — the open world is bounded by the generated 8×6 chunk area.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: World grid tokens (`CHUNK_SIZE` 16, `WORLD_COLS` 6, `WORLD_ROWS` 4, `TERRAIN_SEED`), player/character motion tuning, interaction timing, camera tuning (base/near-pedestal FOV, blend speeds, interaction zoom, `CAMERA_FAR`), and atmosphere tokens (bright cartoon sky `#bfe3f5`, fog near/far, sun position). There is no map-boundary clamp.
  - `lib/Terrain.tsx`: Open-world ground — one height-mapped `PlaneGeometry` mesh per active chunk (always an 8×6 = 48-chunk window). Vertices are displaced by `world.terrain.heightAt`; per-vertex colors blend the biome `low`/`high` palette and a tileable deterministic `1024×1024` nearest-pixel detail texture adds 4× denser micro-grain without increasing terrain geometry. `flatShading` + gentle height quantization give the cartoon voxel look. A large distant plane fills the fogged horizon. Subscribes to streaming revisions; retains existing chunk geometry from `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/world/geometry.ts`, and only mounts newly prepared terrain meshes. Geometry lifetime belongs to the streaming cache; the detail texture/material are disposed on unmount.
  - `lib/Props.tsx`: Shared instanced geometry for broadleaf/columnar trees and tiered firs with roots, branching trunks and crown highlights; irregular faceted rocks with satellite stones and moss/mineral accents. Fixed-capacity GPU buffers survive streaming shifts; small grass/flower details receive but do not cast shadows. Biome tints color stones and grass. Instance buffers and shared component geometry are disposed on unmount; cleanup calls the prototype disposer because R3F sets the instance `dispose` prop to null.
  - `lib/Pedestals.tsx`: Stone arcade portal frames with emissive inlays, floating snake/letter emblems, compact dark billboard plaques, E badges and pulsing activation rings, reading the mutable hovered id each frame. One permanently mounted point light follows the active pedestal; the light count stays constant when chunks enter/leave, avoiding scene-wide shader recompilation stalls.
  - `lib/WorldStreamer.tsx`: Per-frame bridge from the mutable player position to `updateWorldStreaming(x, z)`. When the player enters a new chunk, the deterministic cache generates missing chunks and publishes a new active 8×6 window; old chunks remain cached but are no longer rendered or collidable.
  - `lib/Crates.tsx`: Streamed interactive supply piles rendered in one fixed-capacity instanced mesh. Mutable block state survives nearby chunk shifts via a weak cache keyed by spawn props; evicted regions respawn deterministically. Sleeping piles beyond 18 units skip physics; moving crates continue settling. Player contact uses actual mutable player height, including flight; teleports suppress player/crate contacts. `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/cratePhysics.ts` handles approach-only impacts, stack transfer, bounce and three-axis tumbling. Instance buffers use the prototype disposer on unmount (compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`); crate geometry is also disposed. Shared procedural geometry has inset wood, framing, braces and nails.
  - `lib/CharacterModel.tsx`: Procedural **voxel scholar-mage** (character `mage`) built entirely from boxes/spheres in code, uniformly scaled `CHARACTER_SCALE` = 1.4. Silhouette: bell-shaped indigo robe (3 stacked skirt boxes + gold belt), dark torso, hooded head with glowing amber eyes, swinging sleeves with skin hands, and a small book held in the left hand. Fully hand-rolled animation in `useFrame`: body bob, skirt sway, forward torso lean, counter-swaying head, arm/forearm swing, and alternating foot steps (each limb is a pivot `group` rotating around its joint axis); gentle idle sway when standing. Movement is unbounded and resolves active-chunk obstacle collisions via the shared `physics.ts`; the body sits on the sampled terrain height (`groundHeight`) and publishes that height for crate collisions; interactions run through the shared `interaction.ts` helpers.
  - `lib/CarModel.tsx`: Voxel car with steering/spinning wheels, driven by `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/carPhysics.ts`. Publishes physical player height for crate collisions; follows spring suspension height and inertial pitch/roll; smoothly aligns to slopes while supported and retains its ground orientation in flight. Slope rollover is gated by ground contact and disabled on purpose-built ramps; movement uses the combined terrain/ramp surface. Teleports reset velocity, chassis attitude and contact state. Uses shared obstacle collision and interaction helpers.
  - `lib/CameraRig.tsx`: Smooth top-down follow camera (exponential lerp on position and look target) that follows the terrain height (`groundHeight + CAMERA_HEIGHT`). Near a pedestal: raises the camera and kicks FOV 50→57. While interacting: **slowly dollies toward the pedestal's billboard name** (much slower blend, `INTERACTION_CAMERA_SPEED` 0.3) and narrows the FOV slightly for a cinematic feel. Writes `state.hoveredPedestalId` and fills the tooltip (label, key hint, target route) from the nearest pedestal within `INTERACTION_RADIUS`.
  - `lib/AutoloopBridge.tsx`: Test-only bridge mounted inside the Canvas by `PortfolioPage`. When `?autoloop=1` is present it exposes `window.__autoloop` (`freeze`, `hideHud`, `setSeed`, `setPlayer`, `stats`) and measures FPS. It also sets `gl.info.autoReset = false` and resets the renderer info each frame, so `stats()` reports the whole-frame draw calls/triangles (postprocessing included) instead of only the last pass. Runtime commands validate numeric input and clean up freeze/HUD state on unmount. `freeze(true)` sets a shared flag (`src/lib/autoloop.ts`) that makes `CharacterModel`, `CarModel`, `Crates`, and `CameraRig` skip their `useFrame` updates, producing a static frame for screenshot capture. `hideHud` toggles the `autoloop-hide-hud` class on `<html>`, which hides elements marked with `data-hud` (see `HubHud`).
  - `lib/state.ts`: Mutable game state ref (player position/facing/walkPhase/targetWalkPhase, interaction timer, `interactionTarget`, `hoveredPedestalId`, tooltip) + `CharacterId` type (`"mage" | "car"`). Player includes physical `y` for crate contacts, written by `CarModel.tsx` and `CharacterModel.tsx`. Initial `facing` is `Math.PI` so the character starts looking **away** from the follow camera.
  - `lib/controls.ts`: Keyboard input (WASD/arrows for movement/steering, E for interaction).
  - `ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`: DOM overlay above the hovered pedestal — ref-driven via rAF (no React re-renders).
  - `ui/HubHud.tsx`: DOM overlay — "PORTFOLIO" title + "3D mini-games" subtitle (top-left) and a WASD / E / 1 controls hint (bottom-center: "движение", "вход в проект", "переключение персонажа"). `pointer-events: none`. Root carries `data-hud` so the autoloop harness can hide it.
  - `ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`: Canvas (fog/sky handled in the scene) + `EffectComposer` (soft `Bloom` + `Vignette`) + HubHud + tooltip + fade overlay + navigation callback. Holds the `character` state (**default `car`**); the **Digit1** key toggles it between mage and car. The fade overlay **slowly brightens to white** during activation (starts at ~25% of the animation, fully white by ~95%) then the route navigates.
- **LettersPage** (`src/pages/Letters`): Route `/letters` — Letter Rain. A full-screen 3D canvas; typing any printable character **anywhere on the page** spawns a single 3D letter above the top boundary. It falls and collides with the side/bottom bounds and with other letters (top stays open). The field is one row deep (`AREA_HALF_D` = 1.5 × letter depth) — letters are locked at z = 0 and pile into a single row. Letters never rotate around Y — they only tilt, and the tilt is **free and dynamic**: letters keep their landed angle, wobble on impact (no restore to a fixed flat pose). Each letter is at least 120×120 px on screen (recomputed from viewport/camera on resize). Cap: `MAX_LETTERS` (9) — the oldest letter is evicted when full.
  - `ui/LettersPage.tsx`: Full-screen layout. Mutable world in a ref; subscribes `letterTyped`/`clearLetters` Effector events and bumps a React counter so the letter list re-renders only on spawn/evict. A single window `keydown` handler (no input field): printable chars → `letterTyped`, Escape → back to the town. A fixed "← Назад" pill button (top-left) navigates back to `/`.
  - `ui/LettersScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — background color, ambient/directional/point lights, floor plane, translucent bounds walls (front/back at ± letter depth), `Letters`, the physics game loop, the camera rig, and Bloom. Camera fits the play box to the viewport each frame and writes `pxPerWorld`/`letterScale` into the world.
  - `ui/Letters.tsx`: One `<mesh>` per letter (drei-style `TextGeometry` from the shared font cache), memoized and keyed by id — old letters are never re-mounted/re-created when new ones spawn, only their transforms are synced from the world each frame. Uses a shared `MeshStandardMaterial` with a soft emissive for Bloom.
- **SnakePage** (`src/pages/Snake`): Route `/snake` — the 3D Snake game. Composes the 3D `<Canvas>` (Arena, Snake, Food, Shards, Effects, CameraRig, lights, Bloom) with DOM overlays (Hud, Screens).
  - `ui/GameLoop.tsx`: Orchestrates the game loop inside Canvas — resets the world when the store's `gameId` changes (new game only, not on resume), steps physics, dispatches score/HP/boost events.
  - `ui/PointerTracker.tsx`: Raycasts the mouse pointer onto the arena floor plane and writes the target point into `inputRef` for steering. Mouse steering is active **only while the left mouse button is held** and is **inverted** (the snake steers away from the cursor); releasing LMB stops steering (WASD keeps working).

## Feature Components (`src/components`)

- **Arena** (`src/components/Arena`): Renders the 3D arena — neon-grid floor (drei `Grid`) and 4 semi-transparent walls with glowing edges.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Wall thickness.
- **Snake** (`src/components/Snake`): Renders the snake as an `InstancedMesh` pool (head + chain), synced from world state each frame. Per-segment colors (head→tail gradient) and orientation.
  - `lib/render.ts`: Per-frame matrix/color sync for the instanced chain. Unused instances (beyond the current segment count) are scaled to 0 every frame to keep them invisible.
- **Food** (`src/components/Food`): Renders "protected" food — translucent shell sphere with a glowing core. Pool of `MAX_FOODS` slots synced from world state.
- **Shards** (`src/components/Shards`): Renders shard pickups as a single `InstancedMesh` octahedron pool (1 draw call). Per-instance colors distinguish edible (amber, from theme) from inert just-broken (gray).
- **CameraRig** (`src/components/CameraRig`): Follow camera behind the head with smoothing, FOV kick on boost, orbit mode on the menu screen.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Camera offsets, FOV values, orbit params.
- **Effects** (`src/components/Effects`): Ambient dust points + a particle burst pool triggered on shell breaks.
  - `lib/particles.ts`: CPU particle simulation (positions/colors/lifetimes, circle sprite texture).
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Particle counts, speed, lifetime.
- **Hud** (`src/components/Hud`): DOM overlay — score, best score, HP hearts, boost bar. The boost bar is ref-driven (updated in a `requestAnimationFrame` loop straight from the mutable world, no React re-renders) and exposed as `role="progressbar"`; hearts carry an `aria-label`. The boost bar dims and shows a `КД X.Xс` countdown while the boost cooldown is active.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for the HUD.
- **Screens** (`src/components/Screens`): DOM overlays for `menu` / `paused` / `gameover` phases — title, rules, controls, play buttons, social links (react-social-icons). Handles Esc-pause and Space/Enter-start hotkeys. Dialogs use `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal` and a Tab focus trap (`useFocusTrap`). Each panel has a "Назад" button that resets to the menu (`toMenu`) and navigates back to `/`.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for screens/panels/buttons.

- **Ramps** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Ramps.tsx`): Three reusable instanced ramp meshes with curved decks, yellow/cream edge markings, direction arrows and wooden supports. Deck and edge-marking heights follow the analytic ramp profile; buffers survive chunk shifts and use the prototype disposer on unmount, compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`.
